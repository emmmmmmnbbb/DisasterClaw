# DisasterClaw 论文下一轮平台包装与 Benchmark 构建修改方案
## 基于当前 `main.pdf` 与本轮讨论整理

> 目标：在不改变当前“task-conditioned active observation / closed-loop measurement”主实验结论的前提下，把 DisasterClaw 从“用于重观测实验的仿真平台”进一步包装为一个完整的研究资源链条：  
> **真实灾害数据 → 地理配准地图场景 → 可交互无人机环境 → 自动构造任务 Benchmark → 闭环 Agent 评测。**
>
> 本轮重点不再继续扩大 T1_TASK 的算法 claim，而是强化三项更稳固的贡献：  
> 1. **Map-anchored georeferenced disaster-world construction**：把 xBD 的静态灾害影像、建筑标注与真实地图空间统一起来；  
> 2. **Programmatically grounded, human-verified task benchmark**：从真实灾害影像与建筑标注中自动构造 ROI-scoped 任务，并经过自动检查与双标注者复核；  
> 3. **Traceable closed-loop UAV inspection platform**：让 UAV state、observation、perception、action 和 terminal answer 在同一地理世界与日志体系中可追踪。
>
> 当前论文已经具备这些实现基础，只是相关内容仍分散在 Section 3、Section 5 和 Appendix A 中，没有形成一个足够清晰的“平台 + Benchmark”叙事。

# 一、当前论文已经具备、应当被重新包装的内容

## 1. 当前 Section 3 已经包含完整的平台基础

当前论文 Section 3 已经明确描述：

- xBD 的 pre-/post-disaster tiles、building polygons、damage labels 与 geographic metadata；
- pixel-to-geographic / geographic-to-pixel affine transform；
- 以 tile center 建立 local world anchor；

因此，当前论文并不是“只有一个网页界面”，而是已经实现了：

> **从静态遥感灾害数据到具备地理状态、可执行动作与可重复观测的交互式世界表示。**

当前这一贡献在论文中被拆散描述，导致审稿人容易只把它理解成“georeferenced crop + simple simulator”。

# 二、建议重新定义 DisasterClaw 的总体定位

## 2.1 当前不建议继续主打的定位

不建议把 DisasterClaw 主要包装成：

> a new reobservation algorithm

也不建议包装成：

> a high-fidelity UAV flight simulator

原因是：

- T1_TASK 相比 HOLD / raw entropy 的主要区间仍跨 0；
- 当前 observation 仍来自正射影像裁剪与重采样；
- flight dynamics、wind、battery、collision、physical-flight validation 尚未纳入主实验。

---

## 2.2 建议采用的总体定位

建议全文统一为：

> **DisasterClaw is a georeferenced platform and benchmark for studying task-dependent active observation in UAV disaster inspection.**

进一步展开为：

> **DisasterClaw transforms static disaster remote-sensing records into map-anchored interactive scenes, derives task instances directly from registered annotations and geometry, and supports traceable closed-loop evaluation from sensing actions to terminal answers.**

中文可以概括为：

> **DisasterClaw 将静态真实灾害遥感数据转化为具备真实地理锚定、可控观测和闭环任务评测能力的无人机灾害巡检研究环境。**

# 三、建议新增的核心概念：Data-to-World Transformation

这是当前论文最值得强化的新概念。

## 3.1 原始 xBD 是什么

原始 xBD 本质上提供：

- pre-disaster image；
- post-disaster image；
- building polygon；
- damage label；
- geographic metadata。

它们本质上仍然是：

> **static disaster records**

---

## 3.2 DisasterClaw 做了什么

DisasterClaw 将它们转化为：

- 真实地图坐标中的 disaster tile；
- 连续 basemap context；
- 可查询的 building annotation；
- task ROI；
- UAV state；
- target state；
- camera footprint；
- movement action；
- reobservation；
- closed-loop episode。

因此建议在 Introduction 中明确提出：

> **A central challenge is not only how to interpret disaster imagery, but how to transform static remote-sensing records into an environment in which an embodied agent can act, reobserve, and be evaluated.**

紧接着写：

> **DisasterClaw addresses this gap by transforming xBD records into map-anchored georeferenced scenes and deriving task instances directly from registered annotations and geometric relations.**

这两句建议作为 Introduction 中 platform motivation 的核心表述。

## Contribution 1 — Map-anchored disaster-world construction

### 建议英文

```text
1. We develop a map-anchored disaster-world construction pipeline that transforms isolated xBD pre-/post-disaster image records into georeferenced interactive scenes. Disaster imagery, building annotations, task ROIs, UAV states, and movement actions are represented in a shared geographic coordinate system, enabling static remote-sensing records to serve as an environment for closed-loop aerial inspection.
```

## Contribution 2 — Programmatically grounded task benchmark

### 建议英文

```text
2. We construct an ROI-scoped disaster-inspection benchmark covering presence, damage, counting, and spatial-relation tasks. Reference answers are deterministically derived from registered building annotations and geometric relations rather than generated by a language model. The generated tasks undergo automated schema, answer-consistency, geometry, duplication, and event-split checks, followed by independent review by two annotators and adjudication before the final set is frozen.
```

---

## Contribution 3 — Traceable closed-loop evaluation platform

### 建议英文

```text
3. We implement a traceable closed-loop UAV inspection platform that integrates an interactive operator console and a headless benchmark runner over the same geospatial world, perception, action, and logging interfaces. The platform records vehicle state, observations, perception evidence, decisions, executed actions, budgets, and terminal answers, allowing a sensing decision to be traced from movement through evidence change to final task outcome.
```

---

## Contribution 4 — Task-dependent active-observation study

### 建议英文

```text
4. Using the platform and benchmark, we conduct a controlled study of task-dependent active observation over 160 verified tasks and 3,360 policy episodes. The results show that narrower observations can both correct and harm perception outcomes, and that additional observations do not monotonically improve terminal task performance. The task-conditioned policy provides the best observed accuracy–reobservation operating point in the tested sample, while the adjusted intervals appropriately limit claims of general policy superiority.
```

# 六、Section 3 建议如何重写

## 6.1 Section 3.1：从“实现结构”提升到“设计目标”

当前 3.1 主要是 operator console、Flask/Socket.IO、headless runner 等实现描述。建议开头先加入三个设计目标。

### 建议英文

```text
DisasterClaw is designed around three requirements for converting static disaster records into an embodied inspection environment. First, geospatial grounding requires disaster imagery, building annotations, task regions, vehicle states, and actions to share a common geographic reference. Second, closed-loop observability requires an executed action to change the environment state and produce a new observation rather than merely trigger another model inference on the same input. Third, traceable evaluation requires the complete chain from movement and observation to perception evidence and terminal task answer to be recorded for replay and analysis.
```

然后再接当前 React/Vite、Flask/Socket.IO、headless benchmark 的系统实现。

这样从“软件说明”变成“研究平台设计”。

## 6.2 Section 3.2 建议改名

> `3.2. Map-anchored disaster-world construction`

```text
The first step of DisasterClaw is a data-to-world transformation that converts isolated xBD disaster records into map-anchored interactive scenes. Each source record contains paired pre-/post-disaster imagery, building polygons, damage labels, and geographic metadata. # 七、新增独立 Section 4：DisasterClaw benchmark construction
```

这是这一轮最重要的结构性修改。

## 7.1 Section 4.1 Source disaster records and candidate ROI selection

### 建议正文

```text
The task benchmark is constructed from xBD post-disaster tiles that retain geographic registration and building-level annotations. A tile is considered as a candidate task region only when its registered imagery provides sufficient valid coverage of the intended ROI; the construction pipeline uses an approximate minimum real-image coverage threshold of 80%. Each retained candidate preserves the disaster-event identity, tile geometry, building polygons, damage labels, and geographic transforms required to derive task evidence and reference answers.
```

### 注意

“约 80%”如果代码里是固定阈值，建议写精确值；如果不同阶段有容差，不要伪装成严格统一的 0.80。

---

## 7.2 Section 4.2 Event split and final ROI construction

### 建议正文

```text
The final benchmark uses three disaster events: Hurricane Michael, the Moore tornado, and Nepal flooding. Final ROIs are separated from the development ROIs used to design the task rules and observation policies. Event and ROI identities are frozen before the online evaluation and are checked automatically as part of the benchmark audit.
```

### 非常重要的限定

这里不要写：

> `the perception model is event-disjoint`

因为当前 ChangeOS checkpoint 仍存在 prior xBD exposure。

应当明确区分：

- **benchmark / policy-development split**；
- **perception training exposure**。

---

## 7.3 Section 4.3 Programmatic task generation

这一节应成为 Benchmark 部分的核心。

### 建议英文

```text
Four task families are generated automatically from the registered building annotations and ROI geometry.

Presence asks whether the ROI contains a building satisfying a specified damage condition.

Damage queries the damage state of a specified annotated building.

Count asks for the number of damaged buildings within the ROI.

Spatial asks for the direction of the nearest target building relative to the ROI center.

Reference answers are computed deterministically from the registered building positions, damage labels, and geometric relations. No language model is used to generate the reference answer. Each task record stores the question, task type, ROI identity, target-building information when applicable, reference answer, difficulty metadata, and ambiguity flags.
```

### 这一节要强调四类任务代表不同 evidence requirement

可以再加入：

```text
The four task families are intentionally associated with different observation requirements. Presence mainly tests regional coverage, damage emphasizes local target detail, count requires both coverage and multi-object detection, and spatial reasoning depends on preserving geometric context. This diversity is important for evaluating whether a reobservation is useful for the current task rather than merely whether it reduces local classifier uncertainty.
```

这一段和后面 task-dependent 实验结果能够直接对应。

---

## 7.4 Section 4.4 Automated quality control

### 建议英文

```text
Before human review, the generated tasks pass automated validation checks covering schema validity, reference-answer consistency, spatial geometry, duplicate questions, and event/ROI split constraints. Tasks that fail any required check are removed or regenerated before entering the final review stage.
```

如果代码还有：

- coverage check；
- target existence check；
- answer vocabulary check；
- direction-bin check；

也可以逐项加上。

---

## 7.5 Section 4.5 Dual-annotator review and benchmark freezing

### 建议英文

```text
Two annotators independently reviewed all 160 final questions. The review checked task type, target ROI, reference answer, and whether the question could be answered from the registered imagery and annotation evidence. Disagreements were adjudicated before the final set was frozen. The resulting benchmark contains 160 tasks over 44 previously unused ROIs: 48 questions from Hurricane Michael, 51 from the Moore tornado, and 61 from Nepal flooding. The frozen task records, review logs, adjudication records, source identities, prompts, and model-weight hashes are bound by the reproducibility protocol.
```

```text
ThinkGeo and DisasterM3 substantially expand remote-sensing reasoning and disaster-oriented multimodal evaluation, but their primary evaluation unit remains a task over provided imagery. # 十一、当前实验结果如何与 Benchmark 设计形成闭环
```

四种 task family 不能只被描述成“题型”，而应当说明它们代表四类不同 evidence requirement。

| Task | 核心 evidence requirement | 当前实验现象 |
|---|---|---|
| Presence | Regional coverage | HOLD 与 T1_TASK 都为 93.18%，wide view 通常已足够 |
| Damage | Local target detail | 66.67% → 75.76%，局部细化最有帮助 |
| Count | Coverage + multi-object detection | 75.97% → 78.29%，有小幅改善 |
| Spatial | Geometric context | 43.68% → 41.38%，缩窄视野可能伤害上下文 |

这一表格建议放在 Discussion，不一定放在 Benchmark section。

它可以支撑一句：

> **The benchmark is not diversified merely by question wording; its four task families impose different evidence requirements and therefore expose different failure modes of active observation.**
