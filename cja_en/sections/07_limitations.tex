\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
The benchmark has only 44 ROIs in three events. Questions from nearby regions may share image context, and the fixed ChangeOS checkpoint has prior xBD exposure; the present results cannot establish unseen-disaster transfer. The final answer distribution is uneven, especially for count. The shortcut controls show a substantial image-aware gap on this set, but they do not prove that every question requires visual evidence or rule out all unmeasured shortcuts. Human review and deterministic annotation rules support reference construction rather than a claim of universal visual answerability.

The camera ladder resamples orthographic imagery and uses asymmetric pre-/post-disaster processing. It cannot represent a physical descent's new angle, occlusion, acquisition conditions, or motion dynamics. The spatial uncertainty score also conditions on one selected predicted target and does not fully represent uncertainty over which damaged building is nearest; the target switch in Fig.~\ref{fig:help-harm-cases} illustrates this unresolved source of harm. A0\_NO\_RECHECK still permits search, and the policies can differ in subsequent answer calls. The matched-call diagnostic addresses repeated answering at recheck transitions, but it is not a terminal-episode counterfactual; perception may remain nondeterministic even when the fixed pixels are identical, and its 95\% ROI-cluster interval crosses zero. The old-view agreement check changes prompts and seeds, so it does not rule out model variance under a strictly identical call. Development and final questions use distinct tiles and UIDs, but their ROIs and rendered footprints can overlap geographically; this limits claims of spatial independence. Recheck counts exclude search and do not measure total render, perception, generation, or flight cost. Of 3,360 episodes, 42 lack a recorded state after the terminal fly action; this affects complete-motion summaries, not the terminal-answer denominator. The qualitative examples explain observed mechanisms but do not establish that rechecking has a generally positive effect.
